DroneFinder

Drone registration and recovery — prototype

Demo mode · Data stays in this browser
Prototype limitation: a drone ID does not reveal its GPS position. This demo can save a location you enter and can show your phone/browser's location with permission. Live drone tracking requires a compatible tracker or an authorized drone-location integration.

1. Register a drone

Create a demo record and generate a unique DroneFinder ID.

2. Find a registered drone

Search using the DroneFinder ID printed on the label or QR code.

3. Report a drone as lost

Mark a drone as lost using its DroneFinder ID.

4. Update a drone's known location

For this demo, enter coordinates obtained from a real, authorized source. This does not automatically connect to a drone.

“Use my phone's location” records where your phone is, not where the drone is. It can be useful for logging where a searcher is standing.

Map and registered drones

Choose a drone to center the map on its saved coordinates. Locations are only as current as their last update.
